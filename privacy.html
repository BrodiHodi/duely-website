Privacy policy

Your data is yours.

What Duely keeps, why it keeps it, and how to take it with you or delete it. In plain English.

Effective November 11, 2026

The short version

  • Duely stores what you type in, so it can back it up and show it to you on any device.
  • We don't show ads, sell your data, or share it with advertisers or data brokers.
  • Duely doesn't use your location and doesn't include third-party analytics or tracking tools.
  • When you choose to scan a receipt, a document or a photo of the fuel pump, or look up your car's specs, that file or photo or your car's description is sent to an AI service to read it. Nothing is scanned unless you ask.
  • You can export everything, or delete your account and all your data, from inside the app.

Who we are

Duely is made by Leverpoint ("we", "us"). This policy covers the Duely app and this website. If you have a question about it, email us at getduely@gmail.com.

What we collect

Your account. Your email address and a password. We never see your password itself; it's stored in scrambled (hashed) form by our sign-in provider.

What you log. The information you enter about your vehicles and their upkeep:

  • Vehicles: the nickname you give them, make, model, year, mileage and a photo if you add one.
  • Vehicle details you choose to fill in, such as the license plate, VIN, color, tire and oil specs, insurance company and policy number, purchase date and price, and notes.
  • Service records: date, description, mileage and cost.
  • Fuel fill-ups: date, odometer reading, gallons, cost and any note.
  • Expenses: date, category, amount and any note.
  • Reminders: what's due, how often, when it was last done, and a set due date if one was given (for example a document's expiry date).
  • Receipts you attach to a record: a photo or PDF.
  • Documents you keep with a vehicle, such as a registration, insurance card or emissions report: the photo or PDF, its type and name, and its issue and expiry dates.

When you use the AI features. We keep a simple count of your scans and lookups (just the time of each one) so we can apply a fair daily limit.

Settings on your phone. Your notification choices (on or off, time of day, how much warning you want) are saved on your phone only. Reminder notifications are scheduled by your phone itself, so we don't need a push service or a device ID to send them.

What we don't collect. Your location, your contacts, advertising IDs, or your browsing activity. Duely only gets the photos and files you pick or take for it; it can't see the rest of your photo library. Duely has no ads and no third-party analytics.

How we use it

Only to run Duely for you: to save and back up your records, show them on your devices, work out things like miles per gallon and what's due, and let you sign in. We'll also use your email to reply if you contact us, and to tell you about important changes to the service or this policy. We don't send marketing email.

Where it's stored

Your account, records, vehicle photos, receipts and documents are stored with Supabase, the cloud database, file storage and sign-in provider we use. Your data is encrypted when it travels between your phone and our servers. Security rules mean each account can only ever read its own records and files; photos, receipts and documents are kept private and are only shown to you through short-lived links.

Apple delivers the app through the App Store and TestFlight, and may collect information under Apple's privacy policy, such as crash reports if you've chosen to share them with developers.

Who we share it with

Nobody, except the service providers we need to run Duely, who can only use it to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase stores your account, records and files (see above).
  • Anthropic provides the AI (Claude) behind receipt scanning and spec lookups. Only when you tap Scan receipt, Scan document, Scan for details, Snap pump, Look up specs or Get a maintenance plan, we send it what that feature needs: the receipt, document or photos you chose (for Snap pump, the pump display and your dashboard), your car's year, make and model and the details you've already filled in, and, for a maintenance plan or a receipt scan, your reminder names (and, for a maintenance plan, your service history). Anthropic uses it to give us the result. Under its commercial terms, it doesn't use it to train its AI models.
  • Web searches. To look up specs, the AI searches the web for your car's year, make, model and engine. Your VIN, plate, name and email are never put into a web search.
  • NHTSA, the US government's road safety agency, decodes your VIN into your car's exact model and engine when you look up specs. Only the VIN is sent.

We don't sell your information or share it for advertising. We'd only disclose it if the law requires us to, such as a valid court order.

Your choices

  • See and change it. Everything we store is visible and editable in the app.
  • Take a copy. In the app, go to Settings, then Export my data, for a spreadsheet (CSV) of every vehicle, record and reminder. It's free, always.
  • Delete it. In the app, go to Settings, then Delete my account. Your account and everything in it, including photos, receipts and documents, are removed right away. Copies in our provider's routine backups are cleared as those backups expire.
  • Skip the AI. Scanning and lookups only happen when you tap them. Everything else in Duely works without them.
  • Turn off notifications in Duely's Settings, or in your iPhone's Settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have extra rights under laws like the GDPR or California's CCPA, such as the right to access, correct or delete your data. You can use all of them through the app, or by emailing us.

How long we keep it

For as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, we delete your data.

Children

Duely isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has made an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. If the change is significant, we'll tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
