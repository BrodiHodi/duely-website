Common questions

Is my data backed up?

Yes. Everything you log saves to your Duely account in the cloud as soon as you tap Save. If you get a new phone or reinstall the app, sign in with the same email and it's all there.

How do I get a copy of my data?

Open Duely, go to Settings, and tap Export my data. You'll get a spreadsheet (CSV file) with every vehicle, service record, fill-up, expense and reminder. It opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.

How do reminders work?

Open a vehicle and add a reminder, like "Oil change every 5,000 miles or 6 months". Duely goes by whichever comes first, and the stoplight shows where things stand: red for overdue, yellow for due soon, green for all good.

When you log the service, tap Mark done on the reminder and it starts counting again.

Not sure what to track? On a vehicle with no reminders, tap Get a maintenance plan. Duely looks up the maker's schedule for that car and suggests reminders, with where each interval came from. You pick which to add.

How does receipt scanning work?

Open a vehicle and tap Scan receipt. Take a photo (add more pages for a long invoice), pick photos, or choose a PDF. Duely reads it and fills in a service entry, fill-up or expense for you to check before saving, and keeps the receipt with the record. A shop invoice with several jobs becomes one entry per job.

Reading is done by an AI service, so always check what it filled in. Scans and spec lookups are limited to 25 a day per account. See the privacy policy for what's sent and why.

Where do looked-up specs come from?

Look up specs online on a vehicle's Details tab decodes your VIN with the US government's free decoder, then searches the web for things like oil type, tire pressures and wiper sizes. Each value shows the website it came from, and nothing is saved until you tick it. Websites can be wrong, so check anything important against your owner's manual or the sticker in the driver's door.

I'm not getting reminder notifications.

Check two places:

  • In Duely, go to Settings and make sure Reminder notifications is on. You can also pick the time of day and how much warning you get there.
  • On your iPhone, open the Settings app, tap Notifications, then Duely, and make sure Allow Notifications is on.

Mileage-based reminders need your current mileage to be right. Update it from the vehicle's page, or just log a service or fill-up and Duely updates it for you.

How is my miles per gallon worked out?

Duely measures between fill-ups where you filled the tank all the way. If you only fill partway, turn off Filled the tank and those gallons roll into the next full tank. Forgot to log one? Turn on Missed logging the last fill-up and Duely won't count that stretch, so one gap doesn't throw off your average.

Does Duely track my location?

No. Duely never asks for your location, and there's no GPS tracking. Mileage comes from what you enter. More in the privacy policy.

How do I delete my account?

Open Duely, go to Settings, and tap Delete my account. This permanently removes your account and everything in it, and can't be undone, so export your data first if you want to keep a copy.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Duely is starting on iPhone. If you'd like to hear when Android is ready, send us an email.

Still stuck?

Email us and tell us what's going on. A screenshot helps. We usually reply within a couple of business days.
