What it does

Everything the glovebox should have kept.

Log it once, and Duely does the remembering. No clutter, no ads, no guessing where your records went.

Backed up, always

Everything you log saves to the cloud the moment you tap Save. New phone? Sign in and it's all there.

Reminders that fit

Set them by miles, months, or both, and whichever comes first wins. You get a heads-up before it's due and again on the day.

Every vehicle

The daily driver, the truck, the weekend project. Add as many as you own, each with its own history and reminders.

Fuel and real MPG

Log your fill-ups and Duely works out your actual miles per gallon, even when you forget one.

What it really costs

Insurance, registration, tolls, parking, the car wash. See what you've spent this year without a spreadsheet.

Your data, to go

Export every vehicle, record and reminder to a spreadsheet anytime. Handy when you sell the car, or just like having a copy.

At a glance

Red, yellow, green. You already know how to read it.

Every reminder gets a little stoplight, so you can see what needs you in about a second.

  • Overdue"Brake fluid is 12 days past due. Worth getting to this week."
  • Due soon"Oil change is due in about 300 miles. No rush, just a heads up."
  • All good"Nothing due. Your stuff is in good shape."
Straight talk

Built for the people other apps let down.

Lost records, surprise paywalls, apps that stop getting updates. We've read the complaints. Here's what we're promising instead.

  • Your records don't disappear

    Your history lives in your account, not just on your phone. Updates, new phones and reinstalls don't touch it.

    Your data is yours. Getting a full copy will never cost money or take an email to support.

  • No bait-and-switch

    If Duely ever adds a paid plan, the features you already rely on won't get locked behind it.

  • No ads, no tracking

    No ads, no selling your data, and no GPS. Duely never asks where you are. Read the privacy policy.
